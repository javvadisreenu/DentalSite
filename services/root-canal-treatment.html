What is a Root Canal?

A root canal removes infected pulp, cleans the canals, and seals the tooth to save it from extraction — virtually painless with modern rotary tools and anesthesia.

The Procedure

  1. Diagnosis & digital X-Ray
  2. Local anesthesia
  3. Pulp removal & rotary cleaning
  4. Sealing & crown placement
