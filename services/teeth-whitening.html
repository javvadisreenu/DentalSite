What is professional teeth whitening?

Over time, teeth pick up stains from tea, coffee, paan, smoking and simply getting older.

Professional whitening uses dentist-grade gel to lift deep stains and lighten your natural tooth shade by several shades — safely, with your gums protected.

Is whitening right for you?

  • Yellowing from tea, coffee or ageing
  • Stains from smoking or paan
  • Getting ready for a wedding or special event
  • Wanting a brighter smile without changing the shape of your teeth

Whitening works on natural teeth only — it does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings. Grey staining from medicines or a dead nerve may need other treatment. Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy.

Aftercare tips

  • For 48 hours, avoid tea, coffee, red sauces, cola and smoking.
  • Mild sensitivity for a day or two is common — a sensitivity toothpaste helps.
  • Brush twice a day and have a cleaning every six months.
  • Occasional touch-ups with take-home trays keep your smile bright.

Frequently asked questions

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, when done or supervised by a dentist with the right gel strength. Professional whitening does not damage enamel.

Does whitening cause sensitivity?

Some temporary sensitivity is common and usually fades within a few days.

How long do the results last?

Typically one to two years, depending on habits like tea, coffee and smoking. Touch-ups can extend results.

Are store-bought whitening products as good?

Over-the-counter products are much weaker, can give uneven results and may irritate the gums. Professional whitening is faster, more even and safer.
