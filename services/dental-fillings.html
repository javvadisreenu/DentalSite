What is a dental filling?

When decay creates a cavity, the damaged part of the tooth has to be removed and the space filled so the decay can't spread.

We use tooth-coloured composite fillings that bond to the tooth and blend in, so nobody can tell. Small cavities are quick to fix — left untreated, they grow and may later need a root canal.

Signs you may need a filling

  • Sensitivity to sweets, hot or cold
  • A visible hole or dark spot on a tooth
  • Food getting stuck in the same place
  • A rough or chipped edge you can feel with your tongue
  • An old filling that is cracked, loose or leaking

Early cavities often cause no pain at all. Regular check-ups catch them while they are small, quick and inexpensive to fix.

Frequently asked questions

Tooth-coloured or silver filling — which is better?

Composite (tooth-coloured) fillings look natural and bond to the tooth, so less drilling is needed. We use composite for most fillings today.

How long do fillings last?

A composite filling typically lasts 5–10 years or more, depending on its size, your bite and how well you look after your teeth.

Does getting a filling hurt?

No. Small fillings are often done without any numbing, and deeper ones are fully numbed first. You may notice mild sensitivity for a few days afterwards.

Can a filling be done on the same day?

Yes. In most cases we can examine, diagnose and fill the tooth in the same appointment.
