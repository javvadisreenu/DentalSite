What is Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone, where it acts as an artificial tooth root. Over a few months the bone bonds with the implant (osseointegration), and a natural-looking crown, bridge or denture is then fixed on top.

Implants look, feel and work like natural teeth. Unlike a bridge, they don't need the neighbouring teeth to be cut down, and unlike dentures, they don't slip.

Who are implants for?

  • People with one or more missing teeth
  • Denture wearers whose dentures are loose or uncomfortable
  • Teeth that can't be saved and need to be removed
  • Anyone who wants a fixed replacement without cutting nearby teeth for a bridge

You need enough healthy bone and gums to support an implant — bone grafting can help if bone is thin. Uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking can slow healing, so we review your health before planning.

The Procedure

  1. Consultation and CBCT scanWe check your bone and gums and plan the exact implant position in 3D.
  2. Implant placementThe implant is placed under local anaesthesia — usually about an hour per implant.
  3. Healing (osseointegration)Over 3–6 months the implant bonds with the bone. A temporary tooth can be provided in the meantime.
  4. Abutment and crownA connector (abutment) and your custom crown are fixed onto the implant.
  5. Review and maintenanceRegular check-ups and cleanings keep your implant healthy.

Aftercare tips

  • Apply a cold pack and keep your head raised on the first day.
  • Eat soft foods for the first 1–2 weeks and chew on the other side.
  • Don't smoke — it is the biggest risk to implant healing.
  • Brush gently around the implant and use the prescribed mouth rinse.
  • Keep every follow-up appointment.

Frequently asked questions

Is implant surgery painful?

The area is fully numbed, so the procedure itself is not painful. Most patients describe the next few days as milder than a tooth extraction, and it is managed with simple medicines.

How long do dental implants last?

With good home care and regular check-ups, implants can last for decades. The crown on top may need replacing after many years of wear.

Am I too old for implants?

There is no upper age limit. Your general health and bone quality matter far more than your age.

Can I get same-day implants?

For suitable cases, a temporary tooth can be fixed on the same day as the implant. Whether this is right for you is decided after the CBCT scan.
