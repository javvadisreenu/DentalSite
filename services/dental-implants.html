What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants replace missing tooth roots with titanium posts topped by natural-looking crowns — offering a permanent, stable alternative to dentures or bridges.

The Procedure

  1. CBCT scan & implant planning
  2. Guided implant placement
  3. Healing & osseointegration (3–6 months)
  4. Crown fitting & final restoration
