Pricing questions

Is the consultation fee adjusted in the treatment cost?

Please ask at the front desk — our team will explain how the consultation fee applies to your treatment plan.

Do you offer EMI?

Yes. EMI options are available for larger treatments such as implants, crowns and root canals. Ask us for the current plans.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We help with insurance paperwork and claims for eligible procedures. Bring your insurance card and policy details to your visit.

Why is the price a range and not a fixed amount?

Every mouth is different. The final cost depends on factors such as the tooth involved, the materials chosen and how complex the case is. You'll get an exact quote after your examination, before any treatment starts.
