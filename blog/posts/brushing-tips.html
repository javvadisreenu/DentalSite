5 Brushing Mistakes to Avoid

By Smile Care Dental Hospital · Hyderabad

Brushing twice a day is the foundation of good oral health, yet many of us pick up habits that do more harm than good. Here are five common brushing mistakes we see in the clinic, and how to fix them.

1. Brushing too hard

Scrubbing with heavy pressure wears away enamel and irritates the gums, leading to sensitivity and recession over time. Use a soft-bristled brush and gentle circular motions — let the bristles do the work, not your muscles.

2. Brushing too soon after acidic food

Citrus fruit, cola, vinegar-based dressings and wine temporarily soften your enamel. Brushing within 30 minutes can scrub that softened enamel away. Rinse with water instead, and wait about 30–60 minutes before brushing so saliva can re-harden the surface first.

3. Not brushing long enough

Most people brush for under a minute. Dentists recommend two minutes, splitting 30 seconds evenly across each quadrant of the mouth. A timer or electric brush with a built-in timer helps you stay honest.

4. Skipping floss and the inner surfaces

Plaque hides between teeth where bristles can't reach, and on the inner surfaces facing the tongue. Floss or interdental brushes once a day, and give the back of your last molars the same attention as the front.

5. Keeping your brush too long

Frayed, splayed bristles clean poorly and harbour bacteria. Replace your toothbrush or electric brush head every three to four months — sooner if the bristles look worn after illness.

Small adjustments add up. Pair these habits with a six-monthly professional cleaning and check-up, and your teeth will thank you for years to come.
